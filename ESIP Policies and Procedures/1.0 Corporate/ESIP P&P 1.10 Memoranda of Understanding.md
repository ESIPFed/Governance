## Policy and Procedure 1.10 - Memoranda of Understanding

### 1. OVERVIEW
ESIP may establish memoranda of understanding (MoUs) with related organizations that describe areas of collaboration 
between the organizations. The Executive Director shall negotiate the MoU with the other organization in consultation 
with the Board. The president and ED shall approve the final MoU language and the ED shall sign. If the MoU involves 
any financial considerations, the Finance Committee Chair shall also review and approve the final MoU. If the MoU 
provides for formal reciprocal partnership or membership, the external organization shall still go through the regular 
ESIP Partnership application and approval process.
